## Decisions (the spec)

1. **Timestamp noise vs SNR (model).** σ_ts(SNR) = tsNoisePs · sqrt(SNR_ref / SNR_lin), floored at tsNoisePs and capped at 10 × tsNoisePs, with SNR_ref = 20 dB (model; Cramér-Rao shape, tagged "model"). SNR is the frame's rx SINR at the RMARKER instant as the channel already computes it; for an MMS train the combined SNR (heard fragments summed, as `MMS_COMBINE` does) is used. Default scenes keep their hashes only where their SNR ≥ SNR_ref — the task lists every key that moves and why (weak links get worse, never better).
2. **RIF start offset.** RIF-y starts at (X + Z + y − 1) ms per D5.0 (cite the clause in the constant's tag); if D5.0 in the corpus (`D:\ai_patent_experiments\.claude\skills\wifi_patent_skill\references\uwb_tg4ab`) says otherwise, keep the current rule and record the finding instead — the implementer checks before coding.
3. **device.ts split.** `device.ts` (1 577 lines) → `device.ts` (core state, TWR), `device.tdoa.ts`, `device.mms.ts`, `device.report.ts`; pure moves, no behaviour change, fixtures unchanged.
4. **One-to-many MMS.** A round is one initiator (tag) and up to N responders (anchors) from the session; the initiator's RSF/RIF train is heard by all; responders answer in their own NB slots and ranging fragments in per-responder slots (D5.0 one-to-many cycle; cite). Per-radio capture: the medium keeps, per receiver, the strongest overlapping fragment train and applies the existing 5 dB capture rule per fragment instead of per frame; `Emission` gains an `rxId` scope. Config: `UwbMmsCfg.oneToMany: boolean` (default false → existing pairwise behaviour and hashes untouched). Records: `UWB_MMS_TRAIN` gains `responders`; `UWB_RANGE` per responder. Lesson scenes for 4ab switch to one-to-many only in Task 4, with new fixture keys.
5. **Lessons.** `uwb-sts` "A timestamp nobody can fake" (module 12 after uwb-frame? — no: module 11 after uwb-frame; terms STS, relay attack, key; scene: uwb-intro's with an STS-off variant showing a spoofed early arrival — model: a relay that advances the timestamp by a fixed 50 ns, tagged model) and `uwb-capstone` "Locate the phone in this flat" (module 14 last; DS-TWR + AoA + one-to-many MMS in the hallway house with a Wi-Fi neighbour; the learner picks anchors and block timing; project rubric like tier1-project). `uwb-mms` / `uwb-mms-numbers` / `uwb-nba` / `uwb-nba-coexist` gain one-to-many as their base scene and a "pairwise" variant.
